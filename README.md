> Create a 10-slide business presentation titled “Improving Passenger Experience at Airports Using Generative AI” for an airport management audience.
>
> Use a mechanism-first structure. Explain the business problem before discussing benefits.
>
> Include:
>
> 1. Title and presentation objective
> 2. Current passenger-service challenges
> 3. Suitable Generative AI use cases
> 4. How the AI-assisted workflow operates
> 5. A flight-delay communication example
> 6. Expected business benefits
> 7. Risks and limitations
> 8. Human review and governance
> 9. Implementation recommendations
> 10. Conclusion and sources
>
> Explain the workflow as:
>
> Verified Information → Structured Prompt → AI Draft → Human Review → Approval → Passenger Communication
>
> Do not invent statistics, financial savings, airport-performance data, passenger-satisfaction scores, regulatory claims or policy information.
>
> Insert [VERIFY] wherever factual evidence, organisational data or policy confirmation is required.
>
> Use concise bullet points and a professional aviation-business theme.
>
> Use realistic and relevant airport visuals.
>
> Do not use real airline logos, passenger data, fake charts or misleading operational information.
